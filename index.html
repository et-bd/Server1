How to use StayTut

A quick guide for students, tutors and anyone who needs assignment help.

Using the dashboard

Pick one of three actions: Find a Tutor (looking for someone to teach you), Get Tuition (find students and tutoring opportunities) or Assignment Help (get help with assignments and academic tasks).

  1. Open the Dashboard and use the search and filters (type, verified only, faculty, online or offline, money per day, month or semester).
  2. Read the card: course code, faculty initial, grade, mode, language, dates, days, session time, money and status.
  3. Tap View Details for the full details, including tutoring time.
  4. Tap Apply now, fill in your NSU email, WhatsApp number, Facebook link and a photo of your university ID card, then submit.
  5. StayTut contacts you on WhatsApp or Facebook to set up the match.

Post a tutor offer or a tuition job

  1. Open Post and choose I’m a tutor or I need a tutor.
  2. Step 1: add the course, grade, mode, money, dates, days, time and session time. Pick a ready session length or choose Other to type your own minutes.
  3. Press Continue. Step 2: add your private contact details and ID card photo, accept the agreements and submit.
  4. Your post appears after a quick check. To earn the Verified badge, see Referral rewards.

Assignment help

Choose Assignment help in Post for course work like slides, presentations, reports or posters. Assignments have a single price and a deadline. They have no days, sessions or monthly terms.

Referral rewards

After you post you get a referral code. Friends enter it when they post. 5, 10 or 15 valid posts give you 30 days, 70 days or 1 year of Verified. See Referral rewards.

Comments

When you post, add a comment about the job or what you need. Students see it under “Comments from the poster” in View Details.

Contact or report to admin

Use the Contact page for questions. To report a post, open View Details and tap Report this post.

Reading a card

  • Green Verified badge: StayTut has checked this person. A grey “Not verified” label means the person has not been checked yet. Gender preference shows in pink when it is Female.
  • Status: Available or Open means you can apply. Closed means the dates or deadline have passed.
  • Grade: the grade a tutor earned, or the minimum grade wanted from a tutor.
